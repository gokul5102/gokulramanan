# Portfolio

Gokul Ramanan's personal site. It does two jobs: it shows Recruiters proof of work, and it turns Prospects reached through Outreach into Leads for freelance work.

## Audiences

**Recruiter**:
Anyone assessing Gokul for a job: a recruiter, hiring manager or interviewer.
_Avoid_: HR, employer

**Prospect**:
A potential freelance client who has not yet submitted the contact form.
_Avoid_: client, customer, visitor

**Lead**:
A Prospect who has submitted the contact form about freelance work. Recruiter messages and LinkedIn DMs are not Leads.
_Avoid_: inquiry, enquiry, contact

## Paths and outreach

**Outreach**:
A cold email or DM that Gokul sends to a Prospect, linking to a page on the site.
_Avoid_: marketing, campaign

**Hiring path**:
The route from the homepage for Recruiters, leading to experience and the résumé.
_Avoid_: hiring door, funnel

**Project path**:
The route from the homepage for Prospects, leading to the contact form.
_Avoid_: project door, funnel, CTA

## Gokul's work

**Sales teams and clients**:
The people who use the systems Gokul builds at JPMorgan Chase: the bank's internal sales teams and its external clients. On the site, "clients" always means these external clients, never Prospects.
"Trader" is allowed only as the name of a filter field on RFQs and trades, never for the people who use the systems.
_Avoid_: traders, users, the desk

**BWIC**:
A bond auction. The site explains the term as "bond auction" where a page first uses it.
_Avoid_: market event

**Alert**:
A notification about BWICs, sent to sales teams and clients.
_Avoid_: market alert

**RFQ**:
A request for quote on a bond.
_Avoid_: price request

**Visibility policy**:
The rule that decides which data each person can see. A salesperson sees data for all the clients they cover.
_Avoid_: internal policy names, permissions, entitlements

**eTrading platform**:
The platform that supports the BWIC bidding workflow for several asset classes, and the alert system. Sales teams and clients use it.
_Avoid_: price screens

**TBA BWIC grid**:
A separate real-time streaming grid of TBA (to be announced) BWICs. It is not part of the eTrading platform or the bond data platform, and it appears only on the Résumé page.
_Avoid_: live grid, price grid

## Content

**Case study**:
A full page telling the story of one piece of Gokul's work: the problem, what he built, the result, and how he worked.
_Avoid_: project page, portfolio item, story page

**Note**:
A post in the Notes section, labelled either Engineering or Personal.
_Avoid_: blog post, article, writing

**Margin note**:
A short note set in the margin beside the main text: a plain fact about the work, or a personal detail.
_Avoid_: sidenote, annotation, callout, quip
