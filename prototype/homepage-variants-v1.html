How an alert reaches a trader

Case study · Bond auction alerts · 2026

Traders set alerts on bond auctions: tell me when a bond like this comes up, at a size like that. The old service checked them in bulk and fell behind when the market got busy.

I led the server side of the replacement. Each alert is checked on its own against the live stream, its filters can nest AND and OR as deep as the trader likes, and a standby engine takes over if the leader goes down.

§ 1Definitions

Terms from the work above, for readers outside finance.

BWIC
Bid Wanted In Competition. A seller lists bonds and dealers bid on them in a short auction.
RFQ
Request for quote. A client asks dealers for a price on a bond.
TBA
To Be Announced. A forward trade in mortgage bonds where the exact pools are named just before settlement.
AMPS
A high-speed messaging engine that streams market data between services.
